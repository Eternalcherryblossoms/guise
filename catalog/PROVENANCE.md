## Coverage

- Memory tiers covered: 6 GB, 8 GB, 12 GB, 16 GB
- Memory tiers required: 6 GB, 8 GB, 12 GB, 16 GB
- Waivers: none
- Deliberately out of scope:
  - **4 GB** -- Low-end handsets do ship 4 GB, but none received a modern Android release in volume, and a profile is only usable on releases its handset actually received. Revisit together with per-release build entries.
  - **24 GB** -- 24 GB handsets are rare enough that claiming to be one is itself distinctive, which is the opposite of the point. Revisit if that changes.
- **Empty (memory tier x release) cells:** 6 GB on Android 15, 12 GB on Android 11, 16 GB on Android 11, 16 GB on Android 12, 16 GB on Android 13, 16 GB on Android 15, 16 GB on Android 16, 16 GB on Android 17
  - A handset in one of these has no fully compatible entry. That is the
    real coverage measure: the two dimensions can each look complete while
    their intersection is empty.
- Android releases with entries: Android 11, Android 12, Android 13, Android 14, Android 15, Android 16, Android 17

One entry per (device, release). A build belongs to exactly one Android release,
so a profile worn on any other release describes a handset that never shipped --
its build ID carries the wrong date and its security patch the wrong era.
`Compatibility.releaseIssue` surfaces that in the picker.
