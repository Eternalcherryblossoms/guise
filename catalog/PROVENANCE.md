
fingerprint that matches no shipped build is *more* distinctive than a common real

## Coverage

- Memory tiers covered: 6 GB, 8 GB, 12 GB
- Memory tiers required: 6 GB, 8 GB, 12 GB, 16 GB
- **Waived (required but uncovered):**
  - **16 GB** -- No verified 16 GB entry exists. Pixel 9 Pro and 9 Pro XL ship 16 GB, and the generator is meant to take them from Google's own build tables; until that lands, a 16 GB handset -- and one of the two test devices here -- has nothing coherent to wear.
- Deliberately out of scope:
  - **4 GB** -- Low-end handsets do ship 4 GB, but none received a modern Android release in volume, and a profile is only usable on releases its handset actually received. Revisit together with per-release build entries.
  - **24 GB** -- 24 GB handsets are rare enough that claiming to be one is itself distinctive, which is the opposite of the point. Revisit if that changes.
- Android releases with entries: Android 11, Android 12, Android 13, Android 14

One build per device means one release per device. A profile worn on a release
its handset never received is an impossible combination, so releases without an
entry are a gap in the corpus rather than a property of the handset.
